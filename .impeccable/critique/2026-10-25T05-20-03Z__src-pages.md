## Design specificity verdict

**LLM assessment.** Half authored, half interchangeable. Specific: the real team heroes, Gina's portrait and 1993 founding story, the aircraft mark, Gina's own lines, Sienna's article. Interchangeable: the page skeleton (hero, carousel, services, navy Virtuoso band, founder, journal, "Why book", logo wall, newsletter, footer), the stock carousel, the "Why book" list. Components are tidy; the problem is too much repeated material around too little page-specific content.

**Deterministic scan.** 63 file-engine findings over `dist/`, of which 52 are false positives (cramped-padding 38, tight-leading 6, file-engine low-contrast 8) and 10 are the intentional placeholder stripes. The browser engine found four real issues the review partly missed: hero lede contrast (`/virtuoso/` 3.6:1 at 390, fails AA; 4.2:1 at 1440; `/who-we-are/` 4.46:1), an h1 to h3 skip on `/expertise/`, 89 to 94 character lines on `/expertise/` and `/privacy/` against the kit's 68, and one-word last lines in list items on seven routes.

**Where they agree.** Expertise's long-copy column (review: 720px column with dead space; detector: line length). Hero treatment (review: the navy scrim cools warm photographs; detector: the lede still fails contrast). The two point the same way: protect the text locally rather than darkening the whole photograph.

**What only the review caught.** The menu trap, the closing stack, the seven CTA labels, image reuse, the dead-end Zoom link. None is detectable by rule.

## Overall impression

A disciplined brand system carrying a page structure that repeats itself. The single biggest opportunity is subtraction: every marketing page ends in the same roughly 1,850px (desktop) to 3,100px (phone) stack, which is 41 to 47 per cent of Luxury, Virtuoso and Journal.

## What is working

1. Real people lead: team heroes, Gina's portrait and story.
2. Type and brand discipline: Baskerville 400 with Plex, square photography, hairline rows, two-line heroes. The article is the most Belmond-like page.
3. Gaps are honest and visible, and the enquiry form is short and humane.

## Priority issues

**[P1] Mobile menu traps the visitor after scrolling.** `body.menu-open { overflow: hidden }` (`site.css:27`) breaks the sticky header, so the toggle leaves the viewport behind the fixed nav panel (`site.css:819-841`). Fix: layer the header above the panel or inset the panel below it; lock scroll without breaking sticky; show a real Close state; add a regression test at scrollY > 0. Command: `/impeccable harden`.

**[P1] Hero lede fails AA over photographs.** `/virtuoso/` 3.6:1 at 390. Shared scrim `pages.css:34-43`, lede `:67-72`. Fix: a local text-protection gradient behind the text block, not a heavier full-image scrim, which already cools the grading against DESIGN.md. Command: `/impeccable audit`, then `/impeccable colorize`.

**[P1] Repeated material and heavy chrome.** Closing stack on eight routes; mobile top bar takes 24 per cent of the first screen; logos and newsletter offered twice per page. Fix: one-line top bar under 680px; "Why book" on home and Expertise only; logos in the footer only; hide the partners row until partners are supplied (decision needed: see Decisions); newsletter once per page. Command: `/impeccable distill`, then `/impeccable layout`.

**[P1] The main action has seven names and two behaviours.** "Plan a journey" opens a dialog; "Start planning", "Start planning your holiday" navigate; "Set up a Zoom meeting" reaches a TODO box. Fix: one label, one behaviour, phone as the only secondary. Command: `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P2] Type roles drift from the kit.** Expertise intro 28px over ten lines; Gina's best line set as a 13px kicker above the H2; home service-row h3 at 16px, the size of its descriptions; article body and Journal lede in Libre Baskerville, which BRAND.md line 55 reserves for headings. Expertise skips h1 to h3. Lines run 89 to 94 characters. Command: `/impeccable typeset`.
