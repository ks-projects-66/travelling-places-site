## CO2. Home founder section: Gina's face over-zooms on wide screens; use the team photo; rewrite copy

**Screenshot:** ~2000px viewport, `.founder-feature` on `/`.

**Root cause (geometry, not the photo)**
- `pages.css:1013` bleeds the image to the left edge: width = 500px + (100vw - 1200px)/2. So the box

  `object-fit: cover` to crop to a face-height band and upscale it. That is the zoom.
- `sizes="38vw"` under-requests: at 2000px the box is ~900 CSS px, 1800 device px on retina, but the
  largest width generated is 800. So it is also soft.
- Same bleed pattern may exist on other split sections (Luxury/Virtuoso, About story row). Audit all
  `margin-left: calc(-1 * max(...))` bleeds together so the fix is one rule, not one section.

**Fix geometry (site-wide rule)**
- Replace fixed height with `aspect-ratio` locked to the photograph's own ratio, so the frame scales
  proportionally and the crop never changes with viewport width.
- Cap the bleed: grow to the edge up to a max width the source can support at 2x; past that, the
  section stays composed rather than the image growing.
- Correct `widths`/`sizes` to what the box actually renders.

**Image swap**

  `team-outside.jpg` (1037x853). Both already used on Who we are (story row and hero). Using either on

- At ~600-900 CSS px wide on retina either will be visibly soft. Mitigation: cap the frame width to
  what the file can carry until C22 lands; flag C22 as now blocking a home section, not just About.
- Recommend `team-office` (warmer, people at work, says "calm local office" from PRODUCT.md) and
  accept the repeat with Who we are story row, OR swap Who we are story row to Gina's portrait so
  each photo appears once. Owner's call. Leaning: second option (clean B8, Gina's portrait still lives
  on Who we are).
- Caption bar: "Gina Storey / Founder & travel advisor" no longer matches. Becomes "The Travelling

## CO4. Home Virtuoso panel feels unproportioned

**Verbatim:** "something about this doesn't feel right. the way the logo and the sub headers and body
text all sit with each other. im not sure what it is but we need to reconsider if there's a more
elegant way to show this. a more well proportioned way."

**Where:** `/` `.home-virtuoso` right column, `.virtuoso-panel` (`index.astro:99`, `site.css:390-417`).
White slab on navy; mark centred at 410px max; two benefits (h3 + p) from `src/data/virtuoso.json`.

**Diagnosis (what "doesn't feel right" is, concretely)**
1. Two alignment axes in one small box: mark centred, headings and body left-aligned. The eye has no
   single edge to follow.
2. Inverted hierarchy: h3s at subsection-title (22-28px Baskerville) outweigh the VIRTUOSO wordmark,
   whose letters render ~20px in a raster with built-in whitespace. The mark should anchor, the
   heads should serve; it is the other way round.
3. Spacing has no ratio: mark-to-first-head ~64px (2.5rem + 1.5rem), head-to-body ~6px,
   benefit-to-benefit ~28px. The two benefits read as one run-on block; the mark floats detached.
4. Short measure (~40 characters) inside heavy padding gives a choppy rag ("or a unique / shore
   excursion.").
5. A solid white slab on navy is a raised panel in all but shadow. DESIGN.md layout: "no shadows or
   raised panels". Also B8 "marks only as marks": the logo is used as a picture here.
6. Inconsistency: the SAME content renders as a hairline two-column grid on Luxury
   (`.luxury-virtuoso-benefits`, pages.css:1129) and as this centred card on Home. Two treatments of
   one content block.

**Options (to put to owner at recommendations stage)**
- A (lean): remove the white slab. Benefits sit on the navy as two hairline-topped columns in the
  right column (or full width under the heading), same structure as Luxury so both pages share one
  component. Mark goes small and left-aligned at a fixed cap height (like Luxury's 64px), acting as a
  signature, not a picture. Needs a light/knockout version of the Virtuoso mark for navy: only a
  colour PNG is in the repo; vector PDF master is in `FW_ Logos` (MANIFEST note). Check Virtuoso
  brand rules for reversed use. Fallback: keep a small white plate only around the mark.
- B (minimal): keep the panel, fix it: one left axis (mark left, 48-56px cap), spacing on a ratio
  (mark 40 / head 8 / benefit gap 32), hairline between benefits, body at lead size so the measure
  feels intentional, narrower padding so the measure reaches ~50-60 chars.
- C: drop the panel, move the two benefits into the left copy column below the body, mark in the
  footer/trust row only. Simplest, but loses the two-column balance of the band.

**Rules:** DESIGN.md layout (shared edges, hairline structure, no raised panels); BRAND.md type
roles; POLISH-PLAN pass 3 (one focal point) and pass 4 (shared edges); B8 marks only as marks;
POLISH-PLAN rule 1 (one treatment across the site).
**Do with:** check every other use of the Virtuoso mark (footer, Contact trust row, Luxury) in the
same pass so the mark has one size/treatment rule site-wide.
