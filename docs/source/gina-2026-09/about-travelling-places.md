About Travelling Places

At Travelling Places our aim is simple – to help you create your perfect tailor-made holiday, and to organise it flawlessly. It's something we've been doing successfully now for over 30 years. So, whether you’re interested in history, wildlife, culture, adventure or relaxation, our destination specialists will help you arrange every element of your holiday, right down to the smallest detail, and all tailored to suit your own, individual requirements. That, after all, is the beauty of tailor-made travel. You decide where you want to go, how long for, what to see and who to see it with. The rest is taken care of by us.

Travelling Places was established by Gina Storey in 1993 on Tamborine Mountain. Gina’s experience in the industry began in New Zealand and took her to London for 10 years where she worked as a product development manager for British Airways and marketing manager for Australian company Jetset in the UK and Europe. Relocating to Australia Gina put her extensive industry experience into creating a travel agency with a focus on service and creating wonderful experiences for travellers. Over the years Travelling Places has developed a client base all over Australia who have come to the agency for our focus on service.

As a member of Cruise Line International Association (CLIA) we undertake extensive training of cruise lines globally. We sail on and inspect ships regularly to ensure we can give you honest opinions and share our up to date knowledge. Whether you like an intimate sailing vessel, a luxury cruise ship, an expedition to the far reaches of the globe, or a river vessel we would be delighted to help. Gina is recognised as a Global Cruise Icon – there are under 300 in the world so her knowledge is extensive and she loves finding the right experience for you.

Invited to join the global network Virtuoso, Travelling Places is recognised as a member of this exclusive club (the top 1% of agencies globally) and is able to share the benefits of this affiliation with their clients.

It’s a great source of pride that, to this day, Travelling Places remains a privately owned, family business. It's a fact that enables us to put you, our customer, at the heart of everything we do.

Talk to the experts

It's hard to overstate the benefit of talking to an expert when it comes to planning your holiday. All of our advisors have travelled extensively and are delighted to share this first hand knowledge and experience with you. They have an unbiased, informed approach to planning holidays, and will offer suggestions based on first-hand knowledge. We pool our resources so that each client benefits from our collective knowledge. Every itinerary is designed around your individual needs and budget. We undertake constant training and travel regularly  so that you can be assured of up-to-date knowledge of your travel destination.

Personal service

We like to treat our clients as we like to be treated ourselves. Our advisors pride themselves on their level of personal service and will do more than just organise your holiday logistics. Instead they'll take time to get to know you as a traveller. That way they can make informed suggestions as you work together to plan a holiday you'll love. Our aim is simple – to help you create your ideal tailor-made holiday and to organise it flawlessly.

Honest pricing

We believe that it's unfair to just show you 'from' prices that relate to the low season, when you may not want to travel. That's why we take the time to assess your personal itinerary for the dates you want to travel so that we can give you an accurate price for your holiday.
