Luxury Travel with Travelling Places

More than just a holiday

Travelling Places offers extraordinary tailor-made journeys to the world’s finest destinations. But real luxury travel is about far more than just five-star hotels; here are some of the ways that we provide a truly luxurious experience on every step of your journey:

Your own personal travel specialist

Luxury is having an expert travel consultant who knows the region you’re visiting, and can personalise your itinerary into a truly bespoke, tailor-made holiday. Our specialists have all travelled extensively. They have a helpful, informed approach to planning holidays and will offer suggestions based on first hand knowledge and extensive training that Travelling Places invests in. Meet our team.

Truly tailor-made travel

While we offer plenty of travel inspiration on our website and in our brochures, that’s just the beginning. Whether we’re helping you choose that perfect hotel, organising a bespoke sightseeing tour or simply arranging your visas and travel insurance, everything we do is designed to meet your individual needs, making your holiday a truly tailor-made experience. Learn more about tailor-made travel.

An independent company with high standards

As an independent tour company with long-standing worldwide partners and travel advisors that have worked with us for several years , Travelling Places  is able to cater to your every need with a high standard of service unmatched in the industry.

Full support while travelling

Your travel specialist will be on hand not only before your departure, but also while you are on the road, to step in if plans need changing or you need some unforeseen support.
