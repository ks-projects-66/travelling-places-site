# Reference set: advisory peers

Captured 25 October 2026 at 1440px and 390px, home page plus team and contact pages where they
exist. Screenshots are third-party material and are not kept in this repository. The capture ran
from a US address, so regional pop-ups and phone numbers differ from what an Australian visitor
sees; layout does not.

## Why this set

Belmond and Abercrombie & Kent sell inventory: hotels, trains, tours. Travelling Places sells advice
and a relationship. The closest peers are agencies that sell tailor-made planning, with one
benchmark for restraint. Belmond stays the imagery reference (see `DESIGN.md`); this set governs
structure, tone and the path to a person.

| Site | Role in the set | What it is best at |
|---|---|---|
| Jacada Travel | Closest peer | Short path to an enquiry; advisors named by first name |
| Scott Dunn | Peer | Presenting people: named specialists in one matching photographic treatment |
| Black Tomato | Peer | Quiet trust: one monochrome membership strip, Virtuoso included |
| Aman | Restraint benchmark | Three near-identical background tones; text beneath images; contact as a calm list of channels |
| Belmond, A&K | Prior references | Imagery (Belmond); founder quote and live opening hours (A&K) |

## What "simple, yet elegant" means across the set

Each principle is evidenced by at least two peers.

1. One display voice at a modest size ratio, few sizes (Aman, Scott Dunn, Belmond).
2. Three or four close background tones, not five or more (Aman, Belmond, Jacada).
3. Text beneath images, not over them (Aman, Belmond).
4. One primary button, repeated identically; everything else is a text link (Aman, Jacada, Belmond).
5. Quiet text sections alternating with big image sections at uneven intervals (Aman, Belmond, Jacada).
6. People named and shown in one matching treatment (Jacada, Scott Dunn, Black Tomato).
7. Trust kept quiet and placed where the decision is made (Black Tomato, Scott Dunn, Jacada).
8. A short path to a person from any page (Jacada, Black Tomato, Scott Dunn, Aman).

## Anti-patterns the peers show

Self-opening pop-ups. Video heroes that fail (Aman's mobile embed did, during capture). All-caps
condensed type. Uppercase labels over every heading. Decorative rules, icons and textures.
Comparison grids and prices. Award clusters near the top. SEO text blocks on the home page.
Carousels with dot navigation hiding most of their content. Several buttons in the hero. Footers
of 80 to 110 links. Sideways overflow on phones. Confirm-email fields and required budgets. Five
or more background tones.
