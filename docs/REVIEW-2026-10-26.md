## Departures from the review, and why

- The progress marks were first built as a bar that filled over the six seconds. It kept the page
  repainting continuously (measured: the functional suite's script-ready wait slipped by up to two
  seconds, and the "not before 6 seconds" test failed) and a countdown is more urgency than the
  opening wants. The marks are static: the active one solid.
- About's split title was first a forced two-line break in a narrower column, which broke the
  site's two-line heading rule (three lines from 1024px to 1680px, four at 2560px). The column is
  now half the shell plus its margin, and the title wraps balanced: two lines at every width.
