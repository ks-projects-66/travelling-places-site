[Legal name of the business], trading as Travelling Places (ABN [ABN]), respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, hold, use and disclose personal information, in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

## The information we collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you deal with us. It may include:

- your name, email address, phone number and postal address
- the names, dates of birth and contact details of the people travelling with you
- passport, visa and frequent flyer details
- travel preferences, such as dates, destinations, and seat, meal and room requests
- payment details needed to pay for your booking
- emergency contact details
- anything you send us through this website or by email

If you ask us to, we may also collect sensitive information, such as health information relevant to your travel (for example dietary requirements, medical conditions or mobility needs). We collect it only with your consent and only to arrange your travel.

If you give us information about other people travelling with you, please make sure they agree and are aware of this policy.

## How we collect it

We collect most information directly from you: in person, by phone or email, through the enquiry and newsletter forms on this website, and when you book a call with us. Sometimes we receive it from someone else, such as a person booking travel on your behalf, or from a travel supplier during your booking.

You can make a general enquiry without giving your name. To make a booking, we need your details.

## Why we collect it

We use your information to:

- answer your enquiries and plan your travel
- make, manage and change bookings with airlines, cruise lines, hotels, tour operators and other suppliers
- arrange travel insurance, visas and other services you ask for
- send you our newsletter and travel offers, if you have asked for them
- run our business, including accounting, record keeping and meeting our legal obligations
- keep this website working and secure

## Who we share it with

To arrange your travel, we share the information needed with the suppliers that provide it, such as airlines, cruise lines, hotels, tour operators, car hire companies, travel insurers and visa services. We also use service providers to run our business, including email, website hosting, form delivery, scheduling and newsletter services.

We may disclose information where the law requires or allows it, for example to government authorities for border control and security.

We do not sell your personal information.

## Overseas disclosure

When you travel overseas, we share your information with suppliers in the countries you visit and in the countries where those suppliers are based. Some of our service providers also store data overseas, including in the United States and [other countries where our service providers store data].

## Direct marketing

If you sign up for our newsletter or agree to hear from us, we may send you travel news and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in each email, or by contacting us.

## Cookies and links

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics tools. Some third-party services on the site, such as our call scheduler, may set their own cookies.

The website links to other sites, such as Instagram, Facebook and Virtuoso. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

## Security and how long we keep it

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference and loss, and from unauthorised access, change or disclosure. Access is limited to staff who need it. We keep information for as long as we need it for the purposes above and to meet our legal obligations, then destroy or de-identify it.

If a data breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

## Access and correction

You can ask for access to the personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it. Contact us using the details below. We will respond within 30 days, and may need to confirm your identity first. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why in writing.

## Complaints

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
