Enquiry form. If you use the enquiry form, we collect your name and email address (both required), and, if you choose to give them, your phone number, when you are thinking of travelling, who is travelling, and a description of the journey you have in mind. Please do not put passport, payment card or health details in the form. We will ask for what we need later, by a more suitable route.

Newsletter sign-up. If you sign up for our newsletter, we collect your name and email address (both required), and, if you choose to give them, your postal address and phone number. There is also a tick box to opt in to our luxury travel magazine, and we record whether you ticked it. <span class="placeholder-note" data-placeholder>TODO: say what the magazine is, whether it is posted or sent by email, and who produces and sends it.</span>
