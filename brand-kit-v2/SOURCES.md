# Sources and licences

Third-party assets are vendored into this kit so agents use the approved files rather than a library's default appearance.

## Fonts

- Libre Baskerville Regular from `google/fonts`, licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Source TTF, production WOFF2, and licence are included.
- IBM Plex Sans from `google/fonts`, licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Source TTF, production WOFF2, and licence are included.

## Interface icons

Menu, close, chevrons, play, pause, phone, email, and location were harvested from `google/material-design-icons`. The Apache 2.0 licence is included. Only their SVG paths are used. They inherit Travelling Places colour and sizing.

## Social icons

Instagram and Facebook were harvested from `simple-icons/simple-icons`. The CC0 licence is included. Brand names and marks remain subject to their owners' trademark rules.

## Behaviour references

Navigation, carousel, and form semantics follow familiar web conventions and WAI ARIA Authoring Practices. The implementation is local HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no third-party component theme.

## Visual reference

Belmond informs restraint, image priority, scale, and typographic balance only. No Belmond source code, proprietary typeface, wording, photography, or layout has been copied into the kit.
